# Third-party material and license scope

The repository's MIT license covers only original contributions created for The One Screen. It does not grant rights to the game, game-derived artwork/resources, trademarks, or inherited third-party material.

- **Game-derived and inherited artwork/resources:** `assets/`, compiled resources under `load/`, and gameplay screenshots are excluded from the MIT grant.
- **Inherited type/platform declarations and wrappers:** recovered z3D headers under `source/retail/z3D/`, HID/IRRST declarations, retained IRRST IPC code, and SVC wrappers came from the supplied reference materials. No new MIT grant is made for their inherited portions.
- **Texture codecs and atlas utilities:** adapted from supplied mod materials. The MIT grant covers original contributions only and does not relicense inherited portions.
- **Cascadia Code:** the font binary is not bundled. Its retained license notice is `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.
- **Screenshots:** author-supplied gameplay screenshots depict the game and the author's emulator setup; they are excluded from the source-code MIT license.

Where the supplied material did not establish an upstream license, no upstream license grant is inferred.

This project is unofficial and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
